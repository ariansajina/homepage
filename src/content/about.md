If I were born a century ago, I’d be a member of the intelligentsia. Two, and I’d be a priest. I think this is what I and someone I admire have in common. Any time before that, I’d toil the hoe. Today, I toil the keyboard. I’m a naši speaker from Istria, a Balkan émigré, a German immigré, a Yugofuturist(?!), a dancer and other based in Copenhagen.

I break and engage with performing arts. As a performer, I nurture a lasting creative partnership with choreographer Nika Disney. In 2026, I started developing my own practice centred around [30 rounds](/works/30-rounds/). I created and maintain [pleskal.dk](http://pleskal.dk).

Elsewhere, I develop the end-of-day risk engine at Nordea, and have previously worked on individual-based modelling of life history evolution at the [Valenzano lab](https://valenzano-lab.github.io/labsite/). I studied Mathematics at the University of Bonn and Computer Science at ITU Copenhagen.

I am interested in: breaking, dance and choreography, the club dance floor in the tradition of David Mancuso, forming bonds that last, speaking of home over transparent drinks, how to live a good life in a society intent on its own downfall, islands.

If you wish to conspire with me, reach out.
