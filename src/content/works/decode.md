Marco Payer invited me to join his research project as a dancer, assisted by Valentin Alfery. In Marco's own words:

> The research project De|code investigates the elements of a breaking jam through the lens of choreographic methods. At breaking jams, there is no set sequence, such as choreography or script. From a choreographic perspective, a breaking jam is an improvised, collaborative performance by different people of different origins, genders and backgrounds. A jam may seem like a big unorganized chaos to someone from the outside, but for the participants, who can read the score, there are clear structures in which they move freely and express their art.
