In Process two-week research residency at HAUT, Copenhagen: my first author work, made together with four invited artists, all breakers older than thirty (Marco Payer, Tunde Ade, Dung Chinh Nguyen aka Slowz and Connor Gribben), and curator Tijana Mišković.

_30 rounds_ is an emerging ritual in breaking. A breaker turning thirty battles peers in thirty consecutive improvised exchanges of about half a minute each, a rite of passage towards eldership. The narrative of ageing in breaking is still being written, so I ask what a deepening practice can mean as the body's range of motion and strain contract.

At HAUT I treat preparing for this rite as an experimental process, with the studio as both practice space and stage laboratory. Tijana helps with the transcultural dimension of the work, in particular the link between Denmark and the former Yugoslavia.

The residency ended with a worksharing on 3 September 2026. The research continues at [Udviklingsplatformen](https://www.udviklingsplatformen.dk/en/kom-til-worksharings-hos-up), where I adapt the material for a single dancer, together with Marco Payer, and test how a cypher can work as a performance space with one dancer.
