pleskal is an independent, community-driven public calendar for dance and performance art events in Copenhagen, which I built and run.

It gathers the scene in one place, so that people can engage with it without tracking a dozen websites, newsletters and social media feeds, and it gives artists equal visibility regardless of institutional affiliation or production size. Only members can submit events, and any member can invite new ones, so curation is a shared responsibility rather than an editorial decision. Next to that, pleskal scrapes the programs of a few established Copenhagen venues.

It is intentionally small: hyperlocal and niche, with no ambition to scale or become a multi-purpose app. The site and its code are free and open source, a modest contribution to a digital commons free(er) from technofeudalism. It was built playfully, at leisure, in the spirit of _pomalo_, and is inspired by Duk Op and Gancio.
