How can the body keep transforming into a series of fairytale-like creatures? This is the question Nika Janković Disney landed on when she entered the studio with Arian to develop a choreography for a short solo piece, which in retrospect marked the beginning of an ongoing collaboration between the two.

Starting from Arian's _breaking_ vocabulary, they explore broken down shapes which come to life as discrete non-anthropomorphic characters. The perspectives of both performer and audience are shifted to explore the possibilities of a storytelling body.

The recording was selected for the 2020 online edition of Craft Choreography, a Viennese performance platform curated by Hygin Delimat.
